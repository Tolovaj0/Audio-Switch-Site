Audio-Switch –
One-Click Audio Output Switcher for Windows 11

A tiny Windows 11 tray app that toggles between two audio outputs – speakers, headphones, monitors or a soundbar.

No install, one click, two devices. Simple, portable, reliable.

Speakers⇄Headphones
⬇ Download AudioSwitch.exe
1 · Pick two devicesChoose your outputs on first run. They're remembered.
2 · Left-click to switchThe tray icon shows which device is active.
3 · Right-click for moreSettings, custom icons, start with Windows.

Where's the icon?

Windows 11 hides new tray icons behind the ^ arrow. Click it to find Audio-Switch, then drag the icon onto the taskbar to keep it always visible.
